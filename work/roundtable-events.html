The Referral Navigator runs online roundtables for attorneys and other professionals. As its Marketing Virtual Assistant, I set up each event, scheduled its email reminders, promoted it on LinkedIn, and made the event and member graphics.

Eventbrite events and email scheduling

Every session got its own Eventbrite event. For each one I scheduled batches of email campaigns to registrants, so reminders went out on time without manual sends.
